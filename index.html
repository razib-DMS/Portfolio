03 / PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

EduGrowth: integrated EdTech growth case study.

An integrated Growth Product Manager case study linking product design, go-to-market execution, and analytics to one commercial goal.

ROLE

Growth Product Manager

GOAL

Reduce high early student drop-off through a more engaging learning experience, efficient acquisition, and evidence-led optimization. A 42% baseline is used only as a hypothetical modeling assumption.

PHASE 01 — PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

Design a learning habit

Situation

In this hypothetical scenario, a 42% early drop-off baseline signals that many learners leave before completing their learning path. Students need timely support and a reason to return regularly.

Task

Reduce drop-off by improving the study experience and making progress visible, while keeping the first release focused and feasible.

Action

Use User Journey Mapping to pinpoint friction. Draft a Product Requirements Document (PRD) for an “AI Study Companion & Gamified Leaderboard,” with user stories, acceptance criteria, privacy guardrails, and MVP scope. Create Figma wireframes for contextual help and next-step prompts, alongside an opt-in leaderboard that rewards consistent progress.

Result

Target a 25% projected retention boost by validating the prototype with learners and measuring repeat study and cohort retention before scaling. This is a projection for the hypothetical case, not a measured result.

PHASE 02 — DIGITAL MARKETING

Launch with an efficient funnel

Situation

A useful feature needs a steady flow of qualified students; broad acquisition can spend budget without bringing learners who activate and stay.

Task

Build a three-month GTM strategy that reaches high-intent learners and turns discovery into sign-up, first study, and repeat use.

Action

Month 1: research student segments, search intent, and SEO keywords; build a content calendar, landing-page messaging, and funnel baseline. Month 2: publish search-led study guides and feature explainers, and test audience-specific acquisition messages. Month 3: scale the strongest organic and paid channels, retarget engaged visitors, and improve onboarding to first study. Track the funnel from visit → sign-up → activation → repeat use → paid conversion.

Result

Model an 18% lower customer acquisition cost (CAC) by improving qualified conversion and reallocating spend toward channels bringing learners who activate and stay. This is a projected scenario target, not campaign performance.

PHASE 03 — DATA ANALYTICS

Connect engagement to revenue

Situation

Product and marketing teams need a shared view of which learner behaviors lead to sustained engagement and commercial value.

Task

Measure the feature’s impact credibly and make the results actionable for product investment and growth decisions.

Action

Query anonymized user logs with SQL/Python, define an A/B test with a control group and guardrail metrics, and compare activation and retention cohorts. Build an interactive Power BI or Tableau dashboard tracking CAC, monthly active users (MAU), conversion, and revenue growth, with filters by acquisition source and student segment.

Result

Model a 15% overall revenue increase as a scenario outcome if the feature improves retention and paid conversion. Validate with the experiment and finance data before reporting it as an actual result.
